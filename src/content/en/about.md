I’m a master’s student in cyberspace security at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Libo Chen in the [Network and System Security Lab (NSSL)](https://nssl.sjtu.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on using large language models for vulnerability discovery and exploitation. I have a strong background in finding and analyzing vulnerabilities, as well as experience developing security agents and preparing data for LLM post-training and evaluation.

I go by **BeaCox**, short for “Be a cox”, as a reminder to steer my own course. I dabble in **CTF** as a pwn player with [Ph0t1n1a](https://ctftime.org/team/55197/).
